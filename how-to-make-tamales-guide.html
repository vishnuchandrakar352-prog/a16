How to Make Tamales

Tamales take time, but none of the steps are hard. The secret is to break the work into stages and, ideally, to invite friends to help fold.

Uno

Soak the husks

Dried corn husks must be softened before use. Cover them with hot water, weigh them down with a plate and leave for at least an hour. Separate the leaves, rinse away any silk and keep them damp under a towel until you need them.

Dos

Make the masa

Masa harina is mixed with warm broth, a little salt and baking powder, then beaten with fat until light. Beat for several minutes: the masa should feel soft, like thick hummus. A small spoonful dropped into cold water should float when it is ready.

Tres

Spread thinly

Lay a husk smooth side up, wide end towards you.

  • Spread a thin, even layer of masa over the upper two thirds.
  • Leave a border at the sides and a long bare tail at the narrow end.
  • A thin layer cooks evenly and stays tender.
Cuatro

Fill and fold

Place a spoonful of filling down the centre of the masa. Fold one long side over the filling, then the other, so the masa seals around it. Fold the bare tail up underneath. The open top lets you stand tamales upright in the pot.

Cinco

Batch and rest

Work in an assembly line: one person spreads, one fills, one folds. Keep finished tamales covered so the masa does not dry out. Leftover filling freezes well, and so do uncooked tamales, ready to steam another day.

consejo

Taste the masa before you spread it. It should be a little saltier than you think, because steaming mutes the seasoning.
